Settings

MOSS-Image models

Pick the size for your graphics card. Full: the 10 GB transformer as released (24 GB cards; on smaller ones Memory: Low stores it in fp8). Q8 (5.5 GB, 12 GB cards) and Q4 (3.2 GB, 8 GB cards) are quantized GGUF files: Q8 is very close to Full, Q4 loses some fine detail. The first download also brings the Qwen3.5 9B text encoder (in a size to match), the tokenizer and the VAE, which all models share. LoRAs work on every size. Downloads resume if stopped.

      Text encoder in use

      You have more than one size of the text encoder. The full one gives the model exactly what it was trained with; a smaller one needs less system RAM and loads faster. Changing it re-encodes your prompts.

      Model folders

      Folders searched for models besides the download folder: a folder holding MOSS-Image-SFT/-style model folders (e.g. venno-trainer's models folder) or single-file transformers. One per line.

      LoRA folders

      Folders listed in the LoRA picker (sub-folders too), besides . Add your trainer's output folder to test each epoch as it is saved; press ⟳ in the top bar to pick up new files.

      Memory

      This is for the Full model files. The small downloads (Q8, Q4: ⚙ Settings → Models) stay as small as their file in either mode: about 5.5 GB of VRAM for Q8 (12 GB cards), 3.5 GB for Q4 (8 GB cards), plus 2 to 3 GB while a 1024 px picture is made.

      Either way the 9B text encoder stays in system RAM (17 GB for the full one, 6 to 8 GB for a small one) and only visits the graphics card a layer at a time when a new prompt is encoded; encoded prompts are reused. Applies from the next picture, no restart needed.
